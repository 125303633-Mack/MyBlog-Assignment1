How I first discovered it?

Back when i was a kid around 2012 I spent a lot of my time either playing videogames or watching youtube. One of the earliest horror type games I decided to watch was Telltale's 'The Walking Dead' and I loved every bit of it. From the characters to the choices, the setting, tone, artstyle and performances I thought all of it was really interseting, especially because of my age and it being one of the first times I got to experience watching a game like that. Around the time the sequel came out I was able to play through it on my own with my own console and I did the same with the rest of the series.

After I found out about the game I quickly found out more about the show. I heard and read great things about it but for some reason it took me a long time to sit down and watch it. I finally sat down to give it the time for me to watch through every season up until that point. I think that the reason I finally gave it a go was because of one of my friends from school had just finished playing through the games. We talked about the different choices and outcomes from our playthoughs and after that we both said we'll watch through the show and talk about it like we were watching it as if it was airing for the first time.

Why I love it?

I think that the first 7 seasons of the show are some of the greatest seasons of television ever made and released. The characters, plots, tone, atmosphere, music choices, characters actions, their consequences, and just the shear brutality of the world is excellent. As weird as it might sound for a gory horror show it became my comfort show if you wanna call it that for a while. Even after finishing all of it I went back and rewatched a lot of my favourite episodes and arcs.
